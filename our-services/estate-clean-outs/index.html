Estate clean out questions

Asked & answered

Do I need to be there?

No — not at all. We specialize in out-of-state clean outs, or just the times you can't be around. We keep you informed every step of the way, and send before & after photos of the work.

What if there are things we want to keep?

No problem — just let us know what you're looking for. Many families keep photos and documents; as our team works the clean out, they keep a keen eye out for anything you've deemed important and set it aside.

How fast can it be done?

Often a single day, depending on the size of the home. If you're working against a closing date, tell us and we'll build the schedule around it.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of work and how extensive the clean out is — sometimes it's clearing a few leftover pieces, sometimes it's emptying a 4,500 sq ft estate. The price all depends on what needs to be done, and it's set before we start.

Do you donate items?

Absolutely — we try our best to limit what reaches the landfill. If you see our team piling things to the side as they work, it's because we drop donations off separately instead of mixing them with the rest of the load.
