Appliance questions

Asked & answered

Do I need to disconnect it first?

Depends — gas lines and anything electrically hard-wired must be handled before we arrive. Plug-and-play units are no problem, and we can disconnect the water as long as there's a proper shut-off valve.

Do you take refrigerators with freon?

Yes — empty or full, doesn't matter. We handle the proper refrigerant disposal and route them to the right facilities, so you don't have to do anything special.

Can you get it down the stairs?

Not only down — we can pull it out of a basement too. Stairs, tight hallways, second floors: our crews bring the dollies, straps, and hands to get it out safely.

Do you take just one?

We can take one or many — just let the team know what you want gone. A single unit is a perfectly normal call, and a full kitchen is too.
