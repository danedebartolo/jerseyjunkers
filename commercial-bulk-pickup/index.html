Common questions

What does the 15% apply to?

Scheduled pickups from your designated bulk-item areas, starting with your first pickup. Any other work is billed at standard pricing.

How is each visit priced?

By the volume removed during that visit, at 15% off our standard volume-based pricing, with a $175 minimum per scheduled visit.

How long is the program?

Six months of recurring service, with one scheduled pickup about every 4 weeks. Your initial pickup counts as Month 1.

What if we cancel early?

If you cancel before completing 6 months, the 15% savings on completed pickups will be billed back.

What do we get after each visit?

Photo documentation of the cleared areas comes with every invoice.
